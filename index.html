About

I'm Chantal, a Bachelor of Commerce (Healthcare Management) student at Humber Lakeshore in Toronto, growing into health-tech product and UX design. I first studied Biochemistry at York University, and later switched to Humber's Pre-Health Sciences pathway, then realized I wanted to shape how healthcare systems and patient-facing software work from the inside.

I've built at 6+ hackathons, which taught me how to work underpressure, communicate well while working alongside different teammates, and turn ambiguous health-tech challenges into working prototypes quickly.

I bring together product strategy, wireframing in Figma, and front-end code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React). Grounded in systems thinking and human psychology, I focus on building digital health tools that are intuitive, humane, and practical for both patients and clinicians. Even though I have a non-technical background, I've always been curious about the tech and design space, and continue to explore it through self-taught learning, and sort of innovation events.
